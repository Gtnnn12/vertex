### 3. Compras: créditos, mejoras de server y Netrex
- **Créditos**: son una **moneda virtual exclusiva de VERTEX**. No tienen valor real fuera de la app, no son canjeables por dinero, y **NO son reembolsables**, salvo error técnico comprobable de la plataforma (doble cobro, acreditación fallida). Para comprarlos se recarga saldo vía PayPal según el flujo dentro de la app ("chat de compra").
- **Mejoras de server (boosts)**: se compran con créditos (100 créditos por mejora). Cada mejora dura **30 días** desde la compra; renovar vuelve a consumir créditos. Los niveles del server dependen del número de mejoras activas; si bajan, el server vuelve a los beneficios de su nivel base **sin perder nada de lo ganado**.
- **Netrex**: suscripción premium según el plan elegido (mensual u otros disponibles en la app). Se puede pagar con dinero o con créditos. La suscripción se puede perder por uso abusivo según lo indicado en Conducta.

Si tienes cualquier problema (compra, sanción, cuenta), contacta con el staff **a través del chat de compra dentro de la app** (modal de Créditos → «Mi compra») o del ticket de soporte correspondiente. ## 🇬🇧 English

### 1. What VERTEX is
VERTEX is a real-time chat and community platform (web and desktop app) where you can create and join "servers", chat via text and voice, and customize your profile. VERTEX is under development (beta): expect bugs, outages and frequent changes.

### 2. Accounts
- You need an account to use VERTEX. You must be of **legal age in your country**, or have **permission from a parent or legal guardian** to use the service.
- You are responsible for keeping your password safe and for all activity under your account. Do not share or sell your account.
- Multiple accounts are allowed, but they must not be used to evade sanctions, impersonate others, or abuse the support (purchase chat) system.

### 3. Purchases: credits, server boosts and Netrex
- **Credits**: a **virtual currency exclusive to VERTEX**. They have no real-world value, cannot be exchanged for money, and are **NON-REFUNDABLE**, except for a provable technical error (double charge, failed crediting). Credits are topped up via PayPal through the in-app purchase-chat flow.
- **Server boosts**: purchased with credits (100 credits per boost). Each boost lasts **30 days** from purchase; renewing costs credits again. Server levels depend on the number of active boosts; when they drop, the server falls back to its base tier benefits **without losing anything already earned**.
- **Netrex**: a premium subscription under the chosen plan (monthly or others available in-app). Payable with money or credits. Subscriptions may be forfeited for abusive usage as described under Conduct.
- VERTEX staff manually verify every top-up through the purchase chat; crediting usually takes a few minutes. A top-up with fake data or manipulated screenshots results in the ticket being rejected and may lead to sanctions.

### 4. Conduct
The following is not allowed on VERTEX:
- Harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing or intimidation.
- Illegal content, unmarked NSFW content, or copyright-infringing content.
- Cheating, exploits, abusive automation, malicious reverse engineering, or attempts to compromise the platform or its users.
- Impersonating staff, or using fake screenshots/receipts in purchase chats.

Sanctions are graded by severity: warning, temporary kick, permanent removal from a server, or an **account ban** (which may include losing that account's credits, boosts and Netrex).

### 5. Service limits
- VERTEX is in **beta**: we do not guarantee uninterrupted availability, indefinite data retention, or compatibility with old versions.
- We may modify, suspend or discontinue features (including prices and benefits of credits, boosts and Netrex) with reasonable notice in-app.
- Active boosts and subscriptions are not financially compensated for temporary outages.

### 6. Account suspension
You may stop using VERTEX at any time. We may suspend or ban accounts that violate these terms. Credits and benefits of an account banned for a violation are not refunded or transferred.

### 7. Dispute resolution
For any issue (purchase, sanction, account), contact staff **through the in-app purchase chat** (Credits modal → "My purchase") or the relevant support ticket. We aim to reply within minutes during active staff hours. If we disagree, the VERTEX team will make the final decision in good faith.
